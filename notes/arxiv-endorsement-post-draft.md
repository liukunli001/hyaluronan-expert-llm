**帖子标题**：Request for arXiv cs.CL Endorsement — Domain-Adapted LLM for Hyaluronan Research

**正文**：

Hello,

My name is Kunli Liu. I am a Senior Research Scientist at Meta working on LLM agents, and I hold a Ph.D. in Chemistry from Michigan State University. This post concerns an independent research project I completed outside my day job.

I am preparing to submit a paper to the **cs.CL (Computation and Language)** category on arXiv: *HA-Expert: A Domain-Adapted Language Model and Evidence-Checked Benchmark for Hyaluronan Research*. The work presents a domain-adapted language model for hyaluronan (hyaluronic acid) research — the field of my doctoral work. It includes:

- A curated corpus of 12,306 full-text papers (~126M tokens) from the hyaluronan literature, publicly released.
- Full-parameter domain-adaptive pretraining and supervised fine-tuning of an 8B open model, plus retrieval-augmented generation that answers with citations.
- A 240-question benchmark (HA-Expert-Bench) whose answers were checked against source-paper evidence, with a four-way ablation of pretraining, fine-tuning, and retrieval against the base model.
- Public release of the model weights, data, and a live demo: https://huggingface.co/spaces/liukunli/ha-expert-demo · code: https://github.com/liukunli001/hyaluronan-expert-llm

My Google Scholar profile: https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=G0CAhlwAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao

As this is my first submission to the **cs.CL** category, arXiv requires an endorsement from an existing endorser. If you are registered as a cs.CL endorser and find the work appropriate after reviewing the manuscript, I would be sincerely grateful for your endorsement. You can complete it through the official arXiv link: [LINK] — or at https://arxiv.org/auth/endorse with the code: **[CODE]**

I am happy to share the full manuscript, code, and supplementary materials with anyone who would like to review the work first.

Thank you very much for your time and consideration.

Best regards,

Independent Researcher (Senior Research Scientist, Meta)
